# Cost of Medical Procedures in Colombia

The number-one reason patients look abroad is cost. In the United States and Canada, a single elective procedure can carry a price tag high enough to reshape a family's finances — or it may not be covered at all. Colombia has become a go-to alternative because the same categories of care are frequently available for **50–80% less**. This guide explains why the savings are so large, how to think about total cost, and roughly what to expect across common specialties.

> **Important:** The figures below are **approximate ranges and illustrative estimates**, not quotes. Real pricing depends on your case, the provider, the facility, and the complexity of your treatment. Always request a written, itemized quote after a proper evaluation.

## Why Procedures Cost Less in Colombia

Lower prices in Colombia are not a sign of lower quality — they reflect structural differences:

- **Exchange rates and cost of living.** Salaries, facility overhead, and supply costs are lower, and a favorable exchange rate stretches North American and European budgets further.
- **Different malpractice and administrative environment.** Much of a U.S. medical bill reflects insurance overhead, administrative layers, and liability costs that are simply smaller in Colombia.
- **Transparent cash pricing.** Medical tourism runs largely on upfront cash pricing, which removes the opaque "chargemaster" markups common in U.S. billing.

## How to Think About Total Cost

Do not compare only the procedure fee. A realistic budget includes:

1. **The procedure and facility fee**
2. **Surgeon and anesthesia fees**
3. **Pre-op testing and post-op follow-up**
4. **Flights** (often USD 300–700 round trip from many U.S. cities)
5. **Lodging during recovery** (budget by the number of nights your procedure requires)
6. **Local transport and meals**
7. **A contingency buffer** for extended recovery or additional care

Even after adding travel and lodging, the all-in total for many patients still lands far below the home-country price of the procedure alone.
